# FAR Systems™

Voice intelligence. Real visibility. Meaningful impact.

FAR Systems is a voice-first platform that turns spoken reflection into clear insight. People speak naturally. The platform listens, structures what was said, and returns reports, milestones, and visibility that organizations and individuals can act on.

It is built for privacy and control. The founder designs the products. Organizations run them with their people. Members complete reflections. Refiners review the knowledge before it is trusted.

## What it delivers

- **A guided Reflection.** A structured conversation, not an open chat. Each product defines the journey a person walks through.
- **Knowledge from voice.** Protocols and AI turn the session into findings: strengths, growth areas, and recommended next steps.
- **Reports people can use.** Branded reports with approval rules, so insight is reviewed before it is shared.
- **A living record.** Continuum keeps milestones over time, so progress is visible beyond a single session.
- **An operating picture.** Founders see the whole platform. Enterprises see their people, participation, and quarterly capability. Members see their own journey.

The promise on the platform is simple: speak naturally, see clearly, and move forward with confidence.

## How it works

1. **A product is designed.** The founder defines a Reflection: who it is for, the voice protocol, the steps of the journey, how AI behaves, what the report looks like, who may use it, and what it costs. Publishing makes it available.
2. **An organization adopts it.** An enterprise invites people, assigns Reflections, and watches who has started, finished, or fallen behind.
3. **A person reflects.** A consumer or an employee enters the Reflection Room, completes the session, and receives a report.
4. **A refiner reviews.** Generated knowledge waits in a review queue. A refiner checks it, and completed reviews stay in history.
5. **Insight stays in motion.** Notifications call people back when a reflection is due or a report is ready. Analytics, billing, and the financial center show whether the platform is growing and being paid for.

Secure links carry invitations, reports, products, and organizations to the right person, with access rules and expiration.

## Roles

The platform has **five roles**. Each person sees only the work that belongs to them.

| Role | Who they are | What they are responsible for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder | Owner of the platform | Products, protocols, users, AI, links, analytics, and revenue |
| Enterprise | Administrator of an organization | People, assigned Reflections, invitations, participation, reports, quarterly value, and billing |
| Consumer | An individual member | Buying a Reflection, completing it, and following their own progress |
| Employee | A member inside an organization | The same personal journey, assigned through their organization |
| Refiner | A human reviewer | Checking generated reports before they are trusted |

A visitor can learn what FAR Systems is before they sign in. Signing in, creating an account, and recovering a password are available to every role.

## Founder

The Founder Control Center is the operating system of the FAR Platform Engine™.

**Products.** A ten-part design: basic information, the Reflection itself, the voice protocol, the workflow, AI behavior, the report, the dashboard, permissions, pricing, and publish. Safeguards include content filtering, protection of personal information, and an audit of AI activity.

**Building blocks.** The Widget Library holds the pieces of every dashboard, with drafts, versions, and a published release. The Protocol Library holds the voice and behavior schemas those products rely on.

**People.** User Management covers identity, the products and subscriptions a person holds, their access, their security, and an audit of what they have done.

**Knowledge leaving the platform.** Reports control how insight is generated, branded, approved, and delivered. Templates set the look of the document and the rules for sign-off.

**Intelligence.** AI Services shows which models are connected, whether they are healthy, what they cost, and the prompts that guide them. New prompts can be written and saved.

**Distribution.** Link Center creates and watches secure links for invitations, products, organizations, and reports. A founder can see what is active, what expired, and how often links are used.

**The health of the business.** Analytics & Insights covers users, organizations, products, revenue, daily and monthly activity, retention, and where reflections are happening. The Financial Center covers revenue, subscriptions, product sales, enterprise and consumer income, transactions, refunds, and invoices from issue through payment.

**The founder’s own account.** Profile, password, two-factor sign-in, notification choices, trusted devices, and a log of account activity. Alerts arrive for reflections coming due, reports that are ready, milestones, and platform updates.

## Enterprise

An enterprise administrator runs FAR Systems for one organization.

- See the organization at a glance and move into the work that needs attention.
- Keep the employee roster: add people, update roles, and remove access.
- Assign Reflections and track them as organizational work.
- Invite people in and see who has accepted.
- Monitor participation so unfinished reflections are visible.
- Read the organization’s reports.
- Compare Quarterly Capability Value, so one period can be set against another.
- Manage the plan, seats, invoices, and subscription.
- Receive organization notifications and keep an administrator profile with security and activity history.

## Consumer and Employee

These two roles share one journey. A consumer arrives on their own. An employee arrives through an organization. Both are here to reflect and to see what came of it.

- A home that shows the journey, active Products™, finished reports, and what to do next.
- A place to choose a Reflection or product.
- A blueprint that explains the path before it begins.
- Checkout when a Reflection is purchased.
- The Reflection Room, where the session actually happens.
- Reports from completed sessions.
- Continuum, the long record of milestones.
- A personal profile and a notification list.

## Refiner

A refiner is the human check on generated knowledge.

- A home for the review workload.
- A queue of reports waiting to be read.
- A review of each report.
- A history of reviews already completed.
- Profile and settings for security and how they are notified.

## What every role can count on

- A private account: profile, security, and a confirmed sign-out.
- Notifications when something needs attention, with a way to mark items read or come back to them later.
- Search and filters on the lists they work in, and a clear empty state when nothing matches.
- Exports of links, invoices, analytics, and financial summaries when a role is allowed to take information out of the platform.
- Confirmation before a destructive step, such as signing out, turning off two-factor sign-in, ending a device session, or removing an employee.
